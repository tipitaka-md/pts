PART I

PĀRĀYANAVAGGA.

1. Vatthugāthā.

Sn_V,1.1(=976): Kosalānaṃ purā rammā agamā Dakkhiṇāpathaṃ ākiñcaññaṃ patthayāno brāhmaṇo mantapāragū.

Sn_V,1.2(=977): So Assakassa visaye Muḷakassa[^001-1] samāsane vasī Godhāvarīkūle uñchena ca phalena ca.

Sn_V,1.3(=978): Tass'; eva[^001-2] upanissāya gāmo ca vipulo ahu[^001-3], tato jātena āyena mahāyaññam akappayi,

Sn_V,1.4(=979): Mahāyaññaṃ yajitvāna puna pāvisi assamaṃ. Tasmiṃ patipaviṭṭhamhi añño āgañchi[^001-4] brāhmaṇo

Sn_V,1.5(=980): Ugghaṭṭapādo tasito paṅkadanto rajassiro, so ca naṃ upasaṅkamma satāni pañca yācati.

Sn_V,1.7(=981): Tam enaṃ Bāvarī disvā āsanena nimantayi, sukhañ ca kusalaṃ pucchi, idaṃ vacanam abravi[^001-5]:

Sn_V,1.7(=982): "Yaṃ kho mamaṃ[^001-6] deyyadhammaṃ sabbaṃ vissajjitam mayā, anujānāhi me brahme, n'; atthi pañca satāni me."

Sn_V,1.8(=983): "Sace me yācamānassa bhavaṃ n'; ānupadassati, sattame divase tuyhaṃ muddhā phalatu sattadhā."

Sn_V,1.12(=987): "Na so muddhaṃ pajānāti, kuhako so dhan'; atthiko, muddhani muddhapāte vā ñāṇaṃ tassa na vijjati."

Sn_V,1.13(=988): "Bhotī[^002-5] carahi jānāti, tam me akkhāhi pucchitā muddhaṃ muddhādhipātañ[^002-6] ca, taṃ suṇoma vaco tava."

Sn_V,1.14(=989): "Aham p'; etaṃ[^002-7] na jānāmi, ñāṇam m'[^002-8] ettha na vijjati, muddhaṃ muddhādhipāto ca Jinānaṃ h'; eta dassanaṃ."

Sn_V,1.15(=990): "Atha ko[^002-9] carahi jānāti asmiṃ puthavimaṇḍale[^002-10] muddhaṃ muddhādhipātañ[^002-6] ca, tam[^002-11] me akkhāhi devate."

Sn_V,1.16(=991): "Purā Kapilavatthumhā nikkhanto lokanāyako apacco Okkākarājassa Sakyaputto pabhaṅkaro.

Sn_V,1.17(=992): So hi brāhmaṇa sambuddho sabbadhammānapāragū sabb'; ābhiññā-balappatto sabba-dhammesu cakkhumā sabba-dhamma-kkhayaṃ[^002-12] patto vimutto upadhikkhaye[^002-13],

Sn_V,1.18(=993): Buddho so Bhagavā loke dhammaṃ deseti[^002-14] cakkhumā taṃ tvaṃ gantvāna[^002-15] pucchassu, so te taṃ byākarissati."
