# Majjhima Nikāya Sutta 100 (MN 100)

> **Sutta:** MN 100
> **PTS Volume:** Majjhima Nikāya Vol. 2
> **Edition:** Pali Text Society (PTS)

Ko cāhaṃ, bho, ko ca samaṇassa Gotamassa paññāveyyattiyaṃ jānissāmi, so pi nun'; assa tādiso va yo samaṇassa Gotamassa paññāveyyattiyaṃ jāneyyāti. Uḷārāya khalu bhavaṃ Bhāradvājo samaṇaṃ Gotamaṃ pasaṃsāya pasaṃsatīti. Ko cāham[^209-1] bho, ko ca samaṇaṃ Gotamaṃ {pasaṃsissāmi?} {Pasatthapasattho} {ca} so[^209-2] bhavaṃ Gotamo seṭṭho devamanussānaṃ, ye c'; ime, bho, brāhmaṇā pañca dhamme paññāpenti puññassa kiriyāya kusalassa ārādhanāya, cittassa te[^209-3] samaṇo Gotamo parikkhāre vadati[^209-4], yadidaṃ cittaṃ averaṃ abyāpajjhaṃ tassa bhāvanāyāti. Evaṃ vutte Jāṇussoṇī brāhmaṇo sabbasetā vaḷavābhirathā orohitvā ekaṃsaṃ uttarāsaṅgaṃ karitvā yena Bhagavā ten'; añjalim paṇāmetvā udānaṃ udānesi: Lābhā rañño Pasenadissa Kosalassa, suladdhaṃ lābhā rañño Pasenadissa Kosalassa yassa[^209-5] vijite Tathāgato viharati arahaṃ Sammāsambuddho ti. SUBHASUTTAṂ6 NAVAMAṂ.

100. Evam me sutaṃ. Ekaṃ samayaṃ Bhagavā Kosalesu cārikaṃ carati mahatā bhikkhusaṃghena saddhiṃ. Tena kho pana samayena Dhānañjānī[^209-7] nāma brāhmaṇī Caṇḍalakappe[^209-8] paṭivasati abhippasannā Buddhe ca dhamme ca saṃghe ca. Atha kho Dhānañjānī brāhmaṇī upakkhalitvā tikkhattuṃ

buddhassātī[^210-1]. Tena kho pana samayena Saṅgāravo[^210-2] nāma māṇavo Caṇḍalakappe paṭivasati tiṇṇaṃ vedānaṃ pāragū sanighaṇḍukeṭubhānaṃ sākkharappabhadānaṃ itihāsapañcamānaṃ padako veyyākaraṇo lokāyatamahāpurisalakkhaṇesu anavayo. Assosi kho Saṅgāravo māṇavo Dhānañjāniyā[^210-3] brāhmaṇiyā evaṃ vācaṃ bhāsamānāya, sutvā Dhānañjāniṃ brāhmaṇiṃ etad avoca: Avabhūtā ca 'yaṃ[^210-4] Dhānañjāni {brāhmaṇī} parābhūtā ca 'yaṃ[^210-5] Dhānañjānī brāhmaṇī vijjamānānaṃ brāhmaṇānaṃ, atha ca pana tassa muṇḍakassa samaṇakassa vaṇṇaṃ bhāsatīti[^210-6]. Na hi[^210-7] pana tvaṃ, tāta[^210-8] Bhadramukha[^210-9], tassa Bhagavato sīlapaññāṇaṃ jānāsi; sace tvaṃ, tāta Bhadramukha, tassa Bhagavato sīlapaññāṇaṃ jāneyyāsi, na tvaṃ, tāta Bhadramukha, taṃ Bhagavantaṃ akkositabbaṃ paribhāsitabbaṃ maññeyyāsīti. Tena hi, bhoti, yadā samaṇo Gotamo Caṇḍalakappaṃ anuppatto hoti, atha kho me[^210-10] āroceyyāsīti. Evaṃ Bhadramukhāti kho Dhānañjānī brāhmaṇī Saṅgāravassa māṇavassa paccassosi. Atha kho Bhagavā Kosalesu anupubbena cārikaṃ caramāno Caṇḍalakappaṃ tad avasari. Tatra sudaṃ Bhagavā Caṇḍalakappe viharati Todeyyānaṃ brāhmaṇānaṃ ambavane. Assosi kho Dhānañjānī brāhmaṇī: Bhagavā kira Caṇḍalakappaṃ anuppatto Caṇḍalakappe viharati Todeyyānaṃ brāhmaṇānaṃ ambavane ti. Atha kho Dhānañjānī brāhmaṇī yena Saṅgāravo māṇavo ten'; upasaṃkami, upasaṃkamitvā Saṅgāravaṃ māṇavaṃ etad avoca: Ayaṃ, tāta Bhadramukha, so Bhagavā Caṇḍalakappaṃ anuppatto Caṇḍalakappe viharati Todeyyānaṃ brāhmaṇānaṃ ambavane. Yassa dāni tvaṃ, tāta Bhadramukha, kālaṃ maññasīti. Evaṃ bhotīti[^210-11] kho Saṅgāravo māṇavo Dhānañjāniyā brāhmaṇiya paṭisutvā yena Bhagavā ten'; upasaṃkami, upasaṃkamitvā Bhagavatā saddhiṃ sammodi sammodanīyaṃ

kathaṃ sārāṇīyaṃ vītisāretvā ekamantaṃ nisīdi. Ekamantaṃ nisinno kho Saṅgāravo māṇavo Bhagavantaṃ etad avoca:-- Santi kho[^211-1], bho Gotama, eke samaṇabrāhmaṇā diṭṭhadhammābhiññāvosānapāramippattā ādibrahmacariyaṃ paṭijānanti. Tatra, bho Gotama, ye te samaṇabrāhmaṇā diṭṭhadhammābhiññāvosānapāramippattā ādibrahmacariyaṃ paṭijānanti, tesaṃ bhavaṃ Gotamo katamo ti[^211-2]? Diṭṭhadhammābhiññāvosānapāramippattānaṃ[^211-3] ādibrahmacariyaṃ paṭijānantānam[^211-4] pi kho ahaṃ, Bhāradvāja, vemattataṃ[^211-5] vadāmi. Santi, Bhāradvāja, eke samaṇabrāhmaṇā anussavikā, te[^211-6] anussavena diṭṭhadhammābhiññāvosānapāramippattā ādibrahmacariyaṃ paṭijānanti, seyyathāpi brāhmaṇā Tevijjā. Santi pana, Bhāradvāja, eke samaṇabrāhmaṇā kevalaṃ saddhāmattakena diṭṭhadhammābhiññāvosānapāramippattā ādibrahmacariyaṃ paṭijānanti, seyyathāpi {takkī} {vīmaṃsī}. Santi, Bhāradvāja, eke samaṇabrāhmaṇā pubbe ananussutesu dhammesu sāmaṃ yeva dhammaṃ abhiññāya, diṭṭhadhammābhiññāvosānapāramippattā ādibrahmacariyaṃ paṭijānanti. Tatra, Bhāradvāja, ye te samaṇabrāhmaṇā pubbe ananussutesu dhammesu sāmaṃ yeva dhammaṃ abhiññāya, diṭṭhadhammābhiññāvosānapāramippattā ādibrahmacariyaṃ paṭijānanti, tesāham asmi. Tad {aminā} p'; etaṃ, Bhāradvāja pariyāyena veditabbaṃ. Yathā ye te samaṇabrāhmaṇā pubbe ananussutesu dhammesu sāmaṃ yeva dhammaṃ abhiññāya diṭṭhadhammābhiññavosānapāramippattā ādibrahmacariyaṃ paṭijānanti, tesāham asmi. Idha me[^211-7], Bhāradvāja, pubbe va sambodhā anabhisambuddhassa Bodhisattass'; eva sato etad ahosi:-- Sambādho gharāvāso rājāpatho, abbhokāso[^211-8] pabbajjā. Nayidaṃ sukaraṃ agāraṃ ajjhāvasatā ekantaparipuṇṇaṃ ekantaparisuddhaṃ saṅkhalikhitaṃ brahmacariyaṃ carituṃ. Yannūnāhaṃ kesamassuṃ ohāretvā kāsāyāni vatthāni acchādetvā agārasmā anagāriyaṃ pabbajeyyan ti. So kho ahaṃ, Bhāradvāja, apa-

Evaṃ vutte Saṅgāravo māṇavo Bhagavantaṃ etad avoca: -- Aṭṭhita[^212-1] vata[^212-2] bhoto Gotamassa padhānaṃ ahosi, sappurisa[^212-3] vata bhoto Gotamassa padhānaṃ ahosi, yathā taṃ arahato sammāsambuddhassa. Kin nu kho[^212-4], bho Gotama, atthi devā ti? Ṭhānaso me taṃ[^212-5], Bhāradvāja, viditaṃ yadidaṃ atthi devā ti. Kin nu kho, bho Gotama, atthi devā ti puṭṭho samāno, ṭhānaso me taṃ[^212-6], Bhāradvāja, viditaṃ yadidaṃ atthi devā ti[^212-7] vadesi? Nanu[^212-8], bho Gotama, evaṃ sante tucchā musā hotīti? Kissa pana me bhavaṃ Gotamo ādiken'; eva na[^213-4] byākāsīti? Ucce[^213-5] sammataṃ kho etaṃ, Bhāradvāja, lokasmiṃ yadidaṃ atthi[^213-6] devā ti. Evaṃ vutte Saṅgāravo māṇavo Bhagavantaṃ etad avoca: -- Abhikkantaṃ, bho Gotama; abhikkantam, bho Gotama, Seyyathāpi, bho Gotama, nikujjitaṃ vā ukkujjeyya, paṭicchannaṃ vā vivareyya, mūḷhassa vā maggaṃ ācikkheyya. andhakāre vā telapajjotaṃ dhāreyya: Cakkhumanto rūpāni dakkhintīti, -- evam evaṃ bhotā Gotamena anekapariyāyena dhammo pakāsito. Esāhaṃ Bhagavantaṃ Gotamaṃ saraṇaṃ gacchāmi dhammañ ca bhikkhusaṃghañ ca. Upāsakaṃ maṃ bhavaṃ Gotamo dhāretu ajjatagge pāṇupetaṃ saraṇagatan ti. SAṄGĀRAVASUTTAṂ DASAMAṂ.
