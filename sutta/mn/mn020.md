Tassa ce bhikkhave bhikkhuno tamhā nimittā aññaṃ nimittaṃ manasikaroto kusalūpasaṃhitaṃ uppajjant'; eva pāpakā akusalā vitakkā ch. pi d. pi m. pi, tena bhikkhave bhikkhunā tesaṃ vitakkānaṃ ādīnavo upaparikkhitabbo: iti p'; ime vitakkā akusalā, iti p'; ime vitakkā sāvajjā, iti p'; ime vitakkā dukkhavipākā ti; tassa tesaṃ vitakkānaṃ ādīnavaṃ upaparikkhato ye pāpakā akusalā vitakkā ch. pi d. pi m. pi te pahīyanti te abbhatthaṃ gacchanti, tesaṃ pahānā ajjhattam-eva cittaṃ santiṭṭhati sannisīdati ekodihoti samādhiyati. Seyyathā pi bhikkhave puriso sīghaṃ gaccheyya, tassa evamassa: kin-nu kho ahaṃ sīghaṃ gacchāmi, yan-nūnāhaṃ saṇikaṃ gaccheyyan-ti, so saṇikaṃ gaccheyya, tassa evamassa: kin-nu kho ahaṃ saṇikaṃ gacchāmi, yan-nūnāhaṃ tiṭṭheyyan-ti, so tiṭṭheyya, tassa evam-assa: kin-nu kho ahaṃ ṭhito, yan-nūnāhaṃ nisīdeyyan-ti, so nisīdeyya, tassa evam-assa: kin-nu kho ahaṃ nisinno, yan-nūnāhaṃ nipajjeyyan-ti, so nipajjeyya, evaṃ hi so bhikkhave puriso oḷārikaṃ oḷārikaṃ iriyāpathaṃ abhinivajjetvā sukhumaṃ sukhumaṃ iriyāpathaṃ kappeyya; evam-eva kho bhikkhave tassa ce bhikkhuno tesam-pi vitakkānaṃ . . . samādhiyati.
