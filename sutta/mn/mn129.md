Yasmā ca kho, bhikkhave, bālo duccintitacintī ca hoti dubbhāsitabhāsī dukkatakammakārī, tasmā naṃ paṇḍitā jānanti: Bālo ayaṃ bhavaṃ asappuriso ti. Sa kho so, bhikkhave, bālo tividhaṃ diṭṭh'; eva dhamme dukkhaṃ domanassaṃ paṭisaṃvedeti.
