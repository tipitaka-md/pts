Atha kho Esukārī brāhmaṇo yena Bhagavā ten'; upasaṃkami, upasaṃkamitvā Bhagavatā saddhiṃ sammodi sammodanīyaṃ kathaṃ sārāṇīyaṃ vītisāretvā ekamantaṃ nisīdi. Ekamantaṃ nisinno kho Esukārī brāhmaṇo Bhagavantaṃ etad avoca: Brāhmaṇā, bho Gotama, catasso pāricariyā paññāpenti;-- brāhmaṇassa pāricariyaṃ paññāpenti, khattiyassa pāricariyaṃ paññāpenti, vessassa pāricariyaṃ paññāpenti, suddassa pāricariyaṃ paññāpenti. Ko[^178-1] pan'; añño suddaṃ paricarissatīti? Idaṃ kho, bho Gotama, brāhmaṇā suddassa pāricariyaṃ paññāpenti. Brāhmaṇā, bho Gotama, imā catasso pāricariyā paññāpenti. Idha bhavaṃ Gotamo kim āhāti? Seyyathāpi, brāhmaṇa, puriso daḷiddo[^178-4] assako anāḷhiyo[^178-5], tassa akāmassa bilaṃ olaggeyyuṃ[^178-6]: Idaṃ te, ambho purisa, maṃsaṃ khāditabbaṃ, mūlañ ca anuppadātabban[^178-7] ti, -- evam eva kho, brāhmaṇa, brāhmaṇā appaṭiññāye[^178-8] tesaṃ[^178-9] samaṇabrāhmaṇānaṃ atha ca pan'; imā catasso pāricariyā paññāpenti. Nāhaṃ, brāhmaṇa, sabbaṃ paricaritabban ti vadāmi. Na panāhaṃ, brāhmaṇa, sabbaṃ na paricaritabban ti vadāmi. Yaṃ hi 'ssa, brāhmaṇa, paricarato, pāricariyā hetu pāpiyo assa na seyyo, nāhan taṃ paricaritabban ti vadāmi. Yañ ca khvāssa[^178-10], brāhmaṇa, paricarato, pāricariyā hetu seyyo assa na pāpiyo, tam ahaṃ paricaritabban ti vadāmi. Khattiyaṃ ce pi, brāhmaṇa, evaṃ puccheyyuṃ: Yaṃ vā te paricarato pāricariyāhetu pāpiyo assa, na seyyo; yaṃ vā te[^178-11] paricarato pāricariyāhetu seyyo assa, na pāpiyo; kam[^178-12] ettha paricareyyāsīti? Tatr'; idaṃ, bho Gotama, brāhmaṇā suddassa sandhanaṃ paññāpenti, -- asitabyābhaṅgiṃ; asitabyābhaṅgiñ ca pana suddo sandhanaṃ atimaññamāno akiccakārī hoti, gopo va adinnaṃ ādiyamāno ti.
