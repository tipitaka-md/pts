[xxv. Udumbarika-Sīhanāda-Suttanta.] Evam me sutaṃ.

1. Ekaṃ samayaṃ Bhagavā Rājagahe viharati Gijjhakūṭe pabbate. Tena kho pana samayena Nigrodho[^036-1] paribbājako Udumbarikāya paribbājakārāme paṭivasati mahatiyā paribbājaka-parisāya saddhiṃ tiṃsa-mattehi paribbājaka-satehi. Atha kho Sandhāno gahapati divādivass'; eva[^036-2] Rājagahā nikkhami Bhagavantaṃ dassanāya. Atha kho Sandhānassa gahapatissa etad ahosi: ‘Akālo kho tāva[^036-3] Bhagavantaṃ dassanāya, patisallīno Bhagavā, manobhāvanīyānam pi bhikkhūnaṃ asamayo[^036-4] dassanāya, patisallīnā mano-bhāvanīyā bhikkhū; yan nūnāhaṃ yena Udumbarikāya paribbājakārāmo yena Nigrodho paribbājako ten'; upasaṃkameyyan ti.'; Atha kho Sandhāno gahapati yena Udumbarikāya paribbājakārāmo yena Nigrodho paribbājako ten'; upasaṃkami.

2. Tena[^036-5] kho pana samayena Nigrodho paribbājako mahatiyā paribbājaka-parisāya saddhiṃ nisinno hoti unnādiniyā uccāsadda-mahāsaddāya aneka-vihitaṃ tiracchānakathaṃ kathentiyā -- seyyathīdaṃ[^036-6] rāja-kathaṃ cora-

Appasadda-kāmā kho pan' ete[^037-6] āyasmanto, appasadda-vinītā[^037-7] appasaddassa vaṇṇavādino, app eva nāma appasaddaṃ parisaṃ viditvā upasaṃkamitabbaṃ maññeyyāti.' Evaṃ vutte te paribbājakā tuṇhī ahesuṃ.
