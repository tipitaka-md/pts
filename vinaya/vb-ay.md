tena kho pana samayena aññataro bhikkhu Kosambiyaṃ gilāno hoti. ñātakā tassa bhikkhuno santike dūtaṃ pāhesuṃ: āgacchatu bhaddanto mayaṃ upaṭṭhahissāmā 'ti. bhikkhū pi evam āhaṃsu: gacchāvuso ñātakā taṃ upaṭṭhahissantīti. so evam āha: bhagavatāvuso sikkhāpadaṃ paññattaṃ na ticīvarena vippavasitabban ti, ahañ c'; amhi gilāno na sakkomi ticīvaraṃ ādāya pakkamituṃ, nāhaṃ
