Yaṃ cetasikaṃ neva-sātaṃ-nāsātaṃ cetosamphassajaṃ adukkhamasukhaṃ vedayitaṃ cetosamphassajā adukkhamasukhā vedanā: idaṃ vuccati upekhindriyaṃ. Tattha katamaṃ {saddhindriyaṃ}?
